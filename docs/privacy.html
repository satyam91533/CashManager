Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy describes the current intended data handling for Cash Manager. Update this policy before enabling advertising or adding any analytics, account, cloud-sync, or third-party services.

Information you enter

Cash Manager lets you enter transaction details, opening balance, and related cash records. The current app is designed to store this information locally on your device. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or app data may be able to view it. Do not enter passwords, bank PINs, or other highly sensitive credentials.

Sharing and transmission

The app's local cash-book features are not intended to upload your transaction records to a developer server. You may choose to export or share reports yourself using your device's features.

Advertising and third-party services

Google AdMob advertising is planned but is not represented as active on this version of the website. If AdMob is added, this policy must be revised to describe advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location or other data that may be processed by Google, the purposes of processing, consent choices where required, and links to Google's applicable policies. Do not treat this draft as the final policy for an ad-enabled build.

Children

This app is a general-purpose cash-recording utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Security and retention

Records may remain on the device until you delete them or clear app data/uninstall the app. Exported files may remain wherever you save or share them. Keep backups of records you need.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. For questions or privacy requests, contact the project maintainer through the Cash Manager GitHub issues page.
